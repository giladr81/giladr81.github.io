How I work

  • Start with customers and the people who talk to them every day.Before changing Cisco Investigate, I spoke with customers directly and interviewed about 40 customer success managers, account managers and support staff. One finding shaped the redesign: before blocking a suspicious domain, analysts needed to know what blocking it would break, so that became the first thing they saw. Read the case study
  • Don't make customers pay for our internal changes.At AWS, when a new credit calculation service replaced the old one, I worked with engineering on what a forced move would cost: at least a quarter of engineering work, and a worse experience for customers mid-agreement. I chose not to force it. New customers started on the new service, and the old one wound down as existing agreements ended.
  • Make the impact case before the build.On the internal platform for AWS's Migration Acceleration Program, the program team asked for fully automated customer onboarding. I walked them through impact and cost, prioritized a self-service progress view for customers, and launched a small version of the automation first.
