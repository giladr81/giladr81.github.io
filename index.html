Case study, November 2026

Gaia's renewal risk is reliability, not price

Gaia GPS gets more price complaints than onX Offroad, but the bigger gap is the app not working with no signal, and its unhappy reviewers are far more often long-time customers. How I'd measure offline trust and the order I'd fix things in.

→

Why this problem first

Reliability comes before monetization: a better price or paywall only pays off if the people it converts keep renewing, and Gaia's reviews show long-time subscribers turning against the app when it fails them on the trail. It's also a cheap bet to confirm: Outside can check it against crash and offline-failure data it most likely already collects, before building anything new.

Why a B2B PM for consumer apps

My product management work has been B2B, but my users had a job to do under pressure: incident responders mid-investigation at Cisco. Before that, at BillGuard, I built models for consumers trying to spot an unusual charge. An off-roader who loses signal on a forest road is that kind of user: the tool has to work at the moment it's needed. I off-road in BC myself.
